Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains what information KungfuTutor.com ("we") collects and how it is used.

Information stored on your device

Some features — your language choice, selected division and the stations you mark as mastered — are saved in your own browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device and you can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

Member accounts

You can use every lesson without an account. If you choose to create a free member account, you sign in with your email address and a password, or with your phone number and a one-time code sent by text message. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication, which stores your email address or phone number, an encrypted password (never readable by us) and the dates you signed in. Text-message codes are sent by Google; your carrier's normal rates may apply. We use these details only to sign you in and to send account emails you ask for, such as a password reset. We do not sell them. To delete your account, contact us using the details below.

Advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info. For more, see how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors from the EEA, the UK and Switzerland will be shown a consent message before personalised ads are used.

Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics to count page visits. These tools do not tell us who you are.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Contact

Questions about this policy: see our contact details.
