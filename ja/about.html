About KungfuTutor

Last updated: 21 November 2026

KungfuTutor.com teaches movement by showing it. Each lesson pairs an interactive animation — one you can slow down, pause and step through — with a plain-language explanation of how the technique works, the standards it is judged by, and the mistakes people commonly make.

What we cover

  • Traditional kung fu: stances, strikes, kicks and forms
  • Fighting skills from combat sports
  • Self-defence and personal safety
  • Sports skills and fitness racing such as HYROX
  • Mobility, balance and conditioning

How we make lessons

All animations on this site are drawn by KungfuTutor. Rules and specifications are checked against official sources, which we name on each page. We aim to have lessons reviewed by qualified instructors and will credit reviewers by name.
